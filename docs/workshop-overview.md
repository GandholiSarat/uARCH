# Lab Overview

The lab follows a consistent loop:

1. Choose a small workload and fixed input.
2. Instrument or simulate its dynamic execution.
3. Collect a trace or summary.
4. Analyze the data with an explicit model.
5. Compare observations, explain limitations, and ask what experiment would distinguish competing explanations.

Labs progress from instruction and basic-block counts, through branch prediction and cache locality, to dependency-based instruction-level parallelism. The lab directories are templates at this stage; instructions and expected outputs are marked as coming soon.

Students should record commands, versions, inputs, configuration, and observations so results are reproducible. Instructor reference solutions are intentionally not part of this public student repository.
